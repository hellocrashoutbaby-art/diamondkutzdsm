About Your Barber

Rich, Des Moines Master Barber

Every haircut tells a story, and my job is to make sure you leave looking and feeling your best. Whether you're coming in for a clean fade, a sharp lineup, a beard trim, or your kid's first haircut, you'll get the same attention to detail every time you sit in my chair. No rushing, no cutting corners—just quality work and consistency you can count on.

I cut out of Diamond Kutz Barbershop at 3710 6th Avenue in Des Moines, where I focus on giving every client an experience worth coming back for. New faces and longtime clients are always welcome. Booking is simple through this website, and I'll have your chair ready when you arrive. I'm available Tuesday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Mondays and Sundays are my days off, but the rest of the week, I'm here to keep you looking fresh.
